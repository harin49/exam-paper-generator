# Test syllabus

A small syllabus used only by the unit tests. Section ids in square brackets are what
`concepts.json` points to.

## [1] Measurements

**[1(i)]** SI, fps and cgs units with correct symbols; conversion between systems.

**[1(ii)]** Least count, zero error, simple pendulum: time period, frequency, T = 2π√(l/g).

## [2] Motion in One Dimension

Distance and displacement, speed and velocity, acceleration and retardation, velocity-time graphs
and their slope, equations of uniformly accelerated motion.

## [3] Laws of Motion

**[3(i)]** Contact and non-contact forces; units of force.

**[3(ii)]** Newton's laws of motion; linear momentum.
