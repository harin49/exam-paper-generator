# Test syllabus

## 1. Measurements

[S1] (i) SI, fps and cgs units with correct symbols; conversion between systems.

[S2] (ii) Least count, zero error, simple pendulum: time period, frequency, T = 2π√(l/g).

## 2. Motion in One Dimension

[S3] Distance and displacement, speed and velocity, acceleration and retardation, velocity-time graphs
and their slope, equations of uniformly accelerated motion.

## 3. Laws of Motion

[S4] (i) Contact and non-contact forces; units of force.

[S5] (ii) Newton's laws of motion; linear momentum.
